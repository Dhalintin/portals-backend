# Batch 11: email notifications and scheduled announcements

The new separate worker runs scheduled announcement publication and delivers due email outbox jobs through the existing SMTP or Resend configuration. Configure EMAIL_PROVIDER, EMAIL_FROM and the selected provider's credentials as documented in AUTH_EMAIL_ENV.example. General notifications do not require AUTH_EMAIL_KEY or WEB_URL; authentication email delivery still requires them. Build, then run `npm run notifications:worker` as a supervised process alongside the API and the authentication email worker.

The notification worker was not started against the configured live database. Before starting it in staging or production, review existing pending email jobs: the worker can deliver them. WhatsApp, SMS and in-app transport are not implemented and remain pending; email is the supported delivery channel in this batch. EMAIL_PROVIDER=disabled leaves delivery untouched while allowing the worker to publish due announcements.

Apply migration `20261015010000_notification_delivery` before running this version. It adds school-scoped unique event keys, request hashes, leases, retry scheduling and expiry. New manual queue requests to `/api/v1/communication/notifications` require an eventKey of 8–128 characters. Reuse the key for the same message; changed messages return HTTP 409. Choose channel=email with an email recipient to use SMTP/Resend. Outbox inspection requires announcement management permission. `/notifications/process` remains a preview and sends nothing.

Workers claim one job atomically with SKIP LOCKED and a 90-second lease. Claims are school-aware and ignore inactive schools. Provider acceptance marks SENT, meaning the provider accepted the message rather than confirming inbox arrival. Failed delivery retries with exponential backoff starting at 30 seconds, with a maximum of five attempts. Expired jobs are cancelled. New manual jobs expire seven days after their scheduled time; announcement jobs use the announcement expiry or seven days. Stale workers cannot acknowledge a newer claim. Provider errors are stored as generic codes and message contents/recipients are not logged by the preview or worker.

SMTP delivery is at least once: a worker crash after the provider accepts a message can lead to a duplicate on retry. Resend uses a stable provider idempotency key through the shared transport. Keep the worker's transport timeouts below its lease duration. Cancellation prevents unclaimed jobs from being sent, but a message already accepted by a provider cannot be recalled.

Announcement publication, its audit entry and parent email jobs now commit together. Drafts have no queued jobs. Concurrent publishers/schedulers cannot publish or enqueue twice. The scheduler rechecks due time and expiry under the school lock. Recipients are active parent profiles with valid emails in that school, deduplicated by email. Jobs use the content at publication; editing an already published announcement does not send it again. Deleting an announcement cancels pending jobs; delivery rechecks active publication and parent email eligibility before sending. It does not retroactively queue historical announcements or send to staff.

More work remains: connect attendance, result publication and payment events to this transactional outbox, add any desired WhatsApp/SMS provider, review legacy modules, and complete staging/deployment checks. Existing trigger helper methods are not automatic event wiring.

Verification: the regression suite passed 58 tests, the isolated PostgreSQL suite passed 68 tests, and all 25 migrations replayed without schema drift. New checks cover atomic publication, concurrent schedulers, event-key conflicts, tenant isolation, lease ownership, retry limits, expiration and disabled delivery. Email provider calls were mocked; no live messages or migrations were performed.
