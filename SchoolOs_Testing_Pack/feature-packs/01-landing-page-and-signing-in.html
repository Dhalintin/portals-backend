Landing page and signing in

Follow only the cases assigned to you. Record what actually happens, even if the page is confusing or something goes wrong.

Who tests this: Everyone; new school owner

Before starting: A signed-out browser, two controlled email inboxes, and the school address from the coordinator.

Your test details

The web address is from the earlier staging setup. Your coordinator must confirm it. Enter only the app home address, never an invitation/reset link.

How to record each test

Passed: what should happen actually happened. Failed: it did not. Cannot test: you lack the account, data, connection or setup. Not tested: you have not tried it.

After a save, reload the app page to check it stayed saved. Test with invented records only. Never put passwords, activation codes, bank codes, card details or private links in this document.

Try without help first. If you need help, say so: that is useful feedback, even when the feature works.

Continue from a saved backup

For another tester or role, download the current report and backup first.

Answers stay in this browser when saving is available. The original checklist file does not contain your answers.

Register a school

01-03

Use: New owner

Creates a school

Prepare: Use a new test school name and unused inbox. Password: at least 8 characters, including a letter and number.

  1. Open Register.
  2. Enter your name, a controlled email and a new password.
  3. Enter a new test school name and finish.
  4. Sign out and sign back in.

Should happen: One new school opens. You do not have to invent a school code or type a school slug.

Recover a forgotten password

01-10

Use: Staff or parent

Email required

Prepare: A working test inbox. Student passwords are reset through the school, not this email flow.

  1. Choose Forgot password and enter your controlled email.
  2. Open the reset message.
  3. Set a new password and sign in.
  4. Try the old password and reuse the reset link.

Should happen: New password works. Old password and reused link fail. Do not copy the reset link into your report.

Keep separate accounts separate

01-14

Use: Two testers
