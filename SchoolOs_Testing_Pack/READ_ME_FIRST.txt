SchoolOs complete feature testing pack
Prepared 2 November 2026

21 assignable feature packets and 291 numbered test cases.

1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open START_HERE.html in a web browser.
3. Coordinators open coordinator/00-coordinator-guide.html first.
4. Testers open their assigned file in feature-packs.
5. Download the completed report and backup before returning work.

The pack runs locally without installing anything or sending answers to SchoolOs. Use a computer for the checklist if a phone cannot open local HTML; you can still test the actual app on the phone. Paper checklists are also printable.

No live accounts, passwords or database changes were made by creating this pack. Your coordinator must supply controlled test accounts, valid activation codes, the app address and prepared data.
