SchoolOs feature testing pack

Test one feature at a time and tell us what worked, what failed and what felt confusing. Your coordinator will give you the right account and the packet or case numbers to complete.

Start here: extract the ZIP folder first. Open your assigned file from feature-packs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. You do not need to install a testing tool.

Five simple steps

  1. Ask your coordinator for the test app address, account and assigned cases.
  2. Open your packet and fill in your name, role, school and device.
  3. Follow each short test. Tick Passed, Failed, Cannot test or leave Not tested.
  4. Rate how intuitive, easy, beautiful and pleasant the feature felt. Add honest comments and suggestions.
  5. Choose Download completed report and Download backup. Send both downloaded files and any separate screenshots to your coordinator.

The checklist does not send answers to SchoolOs. Answers stay in the browser when permitted; the original checklist file is not your completed report. Download regularly, especially before changing device or browser.

To continue on another device, copy the packet and your backup there. Open Continue from a saved backup inside that packet and choose the backup file.

For paper testing, print the checklist, write your answers and return clear photos/scans. For a filled electronic report, open the downloaded report and use Print or save as PDF.

Keep the test safe and useful

Use the coordinator's invented school and people. Do not change real school records, use real payment cards or transfer money. Only run payment, bank, publication or access-removal tests when the coordinator has prepared them.

Never record passwords, activation codes, email verification codes, card details or complete invitation/reset links. Hide private details in screenshots. If you see another family's or school's private data, stop and contact the coordinator privately.

Cannot test is not a pass. A success message is not enough if the change disappears after reloading. Tests after two days offline must actually be checked after two days.
